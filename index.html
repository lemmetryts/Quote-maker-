Did you know?

Quote trivia worth knowing

📖

Marcus Aurelius never meant to publish anything. His "Meditations" were private notes he wrote to himself as a Roman emperor — historians believe he had no idea they'd still be read two thousand years later.

🎤

Einstein's most quoted line came from an interview, not a paper. His idea that imagination matters more than knowledge was said off the cuff to a journalist in 1929 — not written in any of his scientific work.

🕊️

"Be the change" is a shortened version. Gandhi scholars point out his actual words were longer and more nuanced — the punchy short form was popularized decades after his death.

🏛️

Winston Churchill is one of history's most misquoted people. Historians estimate a huge share of quotes attributed to him online were never actually said by him at all.

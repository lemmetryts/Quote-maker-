Frequently asked questions

Everything people usually want to know before (or after) hitting generate.

What is a quote generator?

A quote generator is a free online tool that instantly creates a quote based on a mood, language, or era you choose — no need to search through books or scroll social media manually.

I need quotes for Instagram, where can I find them?

You can generate free quotes for Instagram captions right on the home page — pick a mood like motivating, sad, or thoughtful, then use the Copy button to paste it straight into your post.

Where can I get random inspirational quotes?

Our motivational quotes generator creates a fresh inspirational line on demand, for free, with no limit on how many you generate.

How do I find sad quotes that match how I feel?

Choose the "Sad" mood in the generator, or head straight to our sad quotes page. Each quote is picked at random, so you can regenerate until one resonates.

Best quotes about life, free and no sign-up?

Yes — this tool needs no account, no email, and no payment. Just pick a mood and generate as many quotes as you like.

Can I choose the language of the quotes?

Yes. The generator currently supports English, French, and Spanish — use the language selector before generating.

Are these quotes from real historical figures?

No — every quote generated by this tool is an original line written for this site, not pulled from a real author. If you're curious about real, famous quotes and their surprising backstories, check out our Blog.

Can I turn a quote into an image to share?

Yes — after generating a quote, tap "Create image" to turn it into a shareable picture with a background of your choice, ready for Pinterest, Instagram, or your story.

What's the difference between "Classic" and "Modern" era?

It's a style filter — Classic-era quotes lean toward timeless, reflective phrasing, while Modern-era quotes reference present-day life and habits.

Is there a limit to how many quotes I can generate?

No limit — generate as many as you want, whenever you want.
